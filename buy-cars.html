Transparency Guide

Understanding Japanese Auction Grades

We only bid on Grade 4.0 and above vehicles so that you receive immaculate cars with original mileage.

Grade 5.0 / 4.5
Near Showroom Condition

Extremely low mileage, flawless interior, original factory paint, no accident history.

Grade 4.0
High Quality Daily Driver

Light normal wear, superb engine and transmission, thoroughly maintained, best value for Ghana.

Grade 3.5
Commercial & Fleet Grade

Higher mileage, solid mechanical foundation, ideal for commercial trotro buses or work pickups.

Grade R / RA
Repaired (We Avoid!)

Past collision damage. Ophei Ansah strictly avoids Grade R unless specifically requested for parts.
