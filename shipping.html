RoRo vs Container Shipping to Ghana

We offer flexible, cost-effective ocean freight tailored to whether you are importing single vehicles or combining vehicles with heavy spare parts.

RoRo (Roll-on / Roll-off) Shipping

Fastest & Most Economical

Ideal for running, single vehicles. Cars are securely driven onto specialized automotive ocean carriers at Japanese ports and discharged directly at Tema Port.

Our 5-Day Tema Port Clearing Process

Why pay exorbitant demurrage and storage fees? Our dedicated wharf staff at Tema Port expedites every step:

Day 1: Manifest
ICUMS Manifest Matching

Electronic Bill of Lading linked to shipping line manifest before vessel docks.

Day 2: Assessment
CCVR Generation

Customs Valuation Report produced and validated against GRA database.

Day 3: Duty Payment
GRA Bank Settlement

Statutory duty, VAT, and levies paid directly to GRA partner banks.

Day 4: Physical Exam
Joint Agency Inspection

Customs, National Security, and GSA physical verification at Golden Jubilee terminal.

Day 5: Release
Gate Pass & Handover

Vehicle driven out of port gates, cleaned, fueled, and handed over to you.
